Quick Start

1. Go to Connection Settings and enter your GitHub and Cloudinary details once — they are saved automatically for future logins.
2. Go to Media Library to upload, replace, or link images and files for every section of your site.
3. After making changes, click Save All Changes to Website at the bottom — your live site updates in 1–2 minutes.
